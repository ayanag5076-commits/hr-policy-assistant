# Lone Star Logistics Employee Handbook (Sample, fictional company)

## 1.1 Pay Schedule
Employees are paid biweekly on Fridays by direct deposit. Pay stubs are available in the employee self-service portal. If a payday falls on a holiday, pay is issued the business day before. Report a missing or incorrect paycheck to Payroll within 3 business days.

## 1.2 Overtime
Non-exempt (hourly) employees earn 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. All overtime must be approved in advance by your supervisor. The workweek runs Sunday 12:00 AM to Saturday 11:59 PM.

## 2.1 Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period based on years of service: 0-2 years: 4.62 hours per pay period (15 days per year); 3-5 years: 6.15 hours per pay period (20 days per year); 6+ years: 7.69 hours per pay period (25 days per year). Part-time employees accrue PTO prorated to scheduled hours. You may carry over up to 40 hours of unused PTO into the next calendar year. PTO balances are shown in the self-service portal.

## 2.2 Requesting PTO
Submit PTO requests in the self-service portal at least 2 weeks in advance for 3 or more consecutive days, and at least 48 hours in advance for shorter requests. Requests are approved by your supervisor based on staffing needs. Unused carryover above 40 hours is forfeited on December 31.

## 2.3 Sick Leave
Employees receive 40 hours of paid sick leave per calendar year, separate from PTO. Sick leave may be used for your own illness, medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member. Notify your supervisor at least 1 hour before your shift starts. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## 2.4 Company Holidays
Lone Star Logistics observes 10 paid holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. Hourly employees required to work on a holiday are paid 1.5 times their regular rate plus holiday pay.

## 2.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees receive up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or in-law) and up to 2 paid days for other relatives. Notify your supervisor as soon as possible.

## 2.6 Jury Duty
Employees summoned for jury duty receive their regular pay for up to 10 working days. Provide a copy of the summons to your supervisor.

## 3.1 Benefits Eligibility
Full-time employees working 30 or more hours per week are eligible for medical, dental, vision, and life insurance on the first day of the month after 30 days of employment. Eligible dependents include a spouse or domestic partner and children up to age 26.

## 3.2 Medical Plans
Two medical plans are offered through BlueBonnet Health (fictional). The PPO Plan has a $1,000 individual deductible, $25 primary care copay, and costs employees $85 per paycheck for employee-only coverage. The HDHP Plan has a $3,000 individual deductible, qualifies for a Health Savings Account (HSA), and costs employees $35 per paycheck for employee-only coverage. The company contributes $500 per year to the HSA of employees enrolled in the HDHP.

## 3.3 Dental and Vision
Dental coverage includes two cleanings per year at no cost and 80% coverage for fillings after a $50 deductible. Vision coverage includes one eye exam per year with a $10 copay and a $150 allowance for frames or contacts every 12 months.

## 3.4 401(k) Retirement Plan
Employees 21 and older may join the 401(k) plan after 60 days of employment. The company matches 100% of the first 4% of pay you contribute. Matching contributions vest 25% per year and are fully vested after 4 years. Change your contribution any time in the retirement portal.

## 3.5 Open Enrollment
Open enrollment runs November 1 through November 15 each year, with changes effective January 1. Outside open enrollment, you may change benefits only within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption, or loss of other coverage.

## 3.6 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
The free, confidential EAP offers 6 counseling sessions per issue per year, plus legal and financial consultations, for employees and household members. Call the EAP line 24/7 at 1-800-555-0199 (fictional).

## 4.1 Attendance and Tardiness
Employees are expected to arrive on time for scheduled shifts. If you will be late or absent, notify your supervisor at least 1 hour before your shift. Three no-call, no-show absences are treated as voluntary resignation.

## 4.2 Remote and Hybrid Work
Office-based roles may work remotely up to 2 days per week with supervisor approval. Warehouse, driver, and on-site operations roles are not eligible for remote work.

## 4.3 Dress Code
Office staff dress business casual. Warehouse staff must wear company-issued safety vests and steel-toe boots on the floor. The company provides a $100 annual boot reimbursement for warehouse employees.

## 4.4 Expense Reimbursement
Submit business expenses with receipts within 30 days through the expense portal. Mileage for approved business travel in a personal vehicle is reimbursed at the current IRS rate.

## 5.1 Leave of Absence and FMLA
Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act. Leave requests are handled individually by HR. Contact HR to start a leave request.

## 5.2 Anti-Harassment and Reporting
Lone Star Logistics prohibits harassment and discrimination. Report concerns to HR, any manager, or the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.

## 5.3 Workplace Safety
Report every workplace injury, near miss, or unsafe condition to your supervisor immediately and before the end of your shift.
